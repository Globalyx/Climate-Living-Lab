## 🌍 Overview

**Climate Living Labs (CLL)** are hybrid environments — both physical and digital — designed to connect communities, technology, and education for regenerative climate action.

Each Lab starts with a simple gesture — a seed, a reused resource, a collective challenge — and grows into a systemic shift through co-design, creativity, and technology.

---

## 🧭 Vision

To create a global ecosystem of **learning, experimentation, and regeneration** that transforms small actions into lasting impact.

---

## 🧩 Structure

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|--------------|
| 🌍 **Hybrid Spaces** | Physical and digital environments where communities test and prototype climate solutions. |
| 🤝 **Co-design** | Collective creation with citizens, innovators, and institutions. |
| 📈 **Scalable Impact** | Expanding local solutions through open knowledge and storytelling. |
| 🧭 **Climate Ambassadors** | 20 regional leaders documenting and facilitating transformation. |
