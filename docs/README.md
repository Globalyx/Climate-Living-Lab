🌿 Climate Living Lab (CLL) & Globalyx Framework
UNICEF Venture Fund Submission: Blockchain for Social Impact
This repository contains the Open Source infrastructure for the Climate Living Lab, a global initiative led by Globalyx to empower youth through climate action, technical sovereignty, and transparent data governance.

🚀 UNICEF Impact Areas
Area 1 (Accountability): High-integrity climate metrics verified via Blockchain and synchronized with UN NAZCA.

Area 2 (Governance): Decentralized, youth-led governance models (DAOs) for local climate labs.

Area 3 (Digital Public Goods): Open-source Carbon Tracking (powered by Klimatkoll science) and LandOS Lite for soil intelligence.

🛡️ Institutional Backing & Leadership
Lead: Carolina Salazar (CEO, Industrial Strategist & Director at Women in Tech Sweden).

Accreditation: Active Member of UN NAZCA (Global Climate Action).

Security: Developed under CyberNord Nordic cybersecurity standards.
