### 🌐 About Carolina
Carolina Hedman is an EU Climate Pact Ambassador, XR & AI expert, and international advisor on technology ethics and climate innovation.  
She has worked for over 20 years connecting education, digital transformation, and sustainability across Latin America and Europe.  
As founder and CEO of Globalyx, she leads the creation of immersive ecosystems — combining extended reality, data intelligence, and social regeneration.  

Her leadership has been recognized by the **European Innovation Council**, **Women in Tech Global**, and **Fintech Americas**.  
Carolina is also an advocate for ethical AI and gender equality in emerging technologies.
