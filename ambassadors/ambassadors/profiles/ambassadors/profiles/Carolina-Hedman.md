### 🌎 Role in Climate Living Labs
Carolina Hedman leads the **Climate Living Labs initiative – From Seed to Shift**, fostering cross-regional collaboration between Europe and Latin America.  
She promotes regenerative innovation through **XR, AI, circular economy, and education for sustainability**, under the European Climate Pact.
