## 🌍 Overview

**Climate Living Labs (CLL)** are hybrid environments where climate education, innovation, and community participation converge to turn small actions into systemic change.

Each Lab bridges **Europe and Latin America**, combining traditional knowledge, technology, and regenerative design.

---

## 🎯 Partnership Purpose

To collaborate in the **co-creation, piloting, and scaling** of Climate Living Labs in alignment with the *From Seed to Shift* methodology — connecting local impact with global learning.
