# 🤝 Memorandum of Understanding (MOU)
### Between Globalyx and [Partner Organization Name]

---

## 🌍 Purpose

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) establishes the framework for collaboration between **Globalyx**, represented by **Carolina Hedman (EU Climate Pact Ambassador, Sweden)**, and **[Partner Organization Name]**, hereinafter referred to as “the Parties”.

The purpose of this MOU is to **co-develop, pilot, and scale Climate Living Labs initiatives** that integrate education, technology, and regenerative action across Europe and Latin America.

---

## 🧭 Objectives

The Parties agree to cooperate in the following areas:

1. **Climate Education and Awareness** – promoting the *From Seed to Shift* methodology through hybrid learning formats.  
2. **Innovation and Experimentation** – applying technologies such as XR, AI, and IoT to test real-world sustainability solutions.  
3. **Local Action** – establishing Living Labs or pilots within the partner’s region or institution.  
4. **Knowledge Exchange** – facilitating research, data sharing, and co-design of community-based programs.  
5. **Visibility and Communication** – sharing stories, case studies, and results across the network.

---

## 🧩 Roles and Responsibilities

| Party | Responsibilities |
|--------|-------------------|
| **Globalyx** | Provides strategic guidance, methodology, and access to the international network of Climate Living Labs. |
| **[Partner Organization]** | Offers local expertise, facilities, and participation in pilots or educational programs. |
| **Both Parties** | Commit to transparency, co-creation, and the open exchange of knowledge. |

---

## 📆 Duration

This MOU will remain in effect for **12 months** from the date of signature, and may be renewed by mutual agreement.

---

## 💚 Principles of Collaboration

- Co-design and shared responsibility  
- Transparency and open data  
- Inclusivity and diversity of participants  
- Mutual respect and acknowledgment of contributions  

---

## 🪴 Expected Outcomes

- At least one **Climate Living Lab pilot** co-designed and implemented.  
- Documented **best practices** and measurable impact indicators.  
- Potential for **scaling or replication** through EU and LATAM collaboration programs.
